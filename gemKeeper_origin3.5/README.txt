	Gem keeper | Extra info :D

- I wanted to add a pro mechanic to a simple origin, so that every player can play this origin.
- The "pro mechanic" is that cooldowns are managed separately. That means that you can change between different gems to ignore a fraction of the cooldown but.. that isn't
 all. The cost of abilities also  has it's own cooldown so, you could use two abilities in a row by changing between gems + ignoring the cost of the second one.

This mechanic makes the emerald mode even more powerful, as it lets you to easily position your gemstone wherever you want. This increases the range of abilities such as
Diamond's and Lapis', which are already powerful by their own.

# Best combos
- Emerald + Diamond / Lapis -> More range and control thanks to the emerald's free positioning + Emerald has a bigger area of effect compared to both diamond and lapis so,
			       this combo would let you to pull entities to the range of effect of Diamond's and Lapis' actives

# Some tips
- Don't waste your light. Without it, you are even worse than a human eww
- Keep always a space for your gems in your hootbar, as you will need to change between them because there isn't a gem better than the others
- Play with the invironment. Emerald's active lets you pull entities to wherever you want. To a pool of lava / cliff.. Your friend and a crowd of mobs to the same place..
- Choose your fighting style on every battle to experiment and have fun! Diamond is a great melee fighter and Lapis a powerful archer. Use Redstone to surprise your foes!
