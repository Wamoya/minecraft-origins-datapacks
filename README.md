# Origins datapacks for Minecraft
> This repo contains several folders. Each of them are an individual minecraft datapack, designed and written by me, that you can use alongside the Minecraft [Origins](https://github.com/apace100/origins-fabric) mod.

## Origins:

### Bad luck cat
**Impact:** 2
**Description:** You are a symbol of bad luck, more exactly, a black cat. A solitary being that uses dark magic while still being cute ^^

> [!NOTE]
> - Coded and tested on 1.16.5
> - Needs pehkui *(search for "minecraft pehkui mod" online)*

***Actives***
- ⌛ **Shadow travel:** You can become a shadow for 3s. Though it’s harder while under direct sunlight.
- 🪄 **Magic missile:** You send a dart of force streaking toward a creature that you can see.
- 📦 **Bag of holding:** You have access to an extra inventory that won’t drop on death.

### The knight
This is an early beta, there are some things missing, but I wanted to upload it anyways

> [!NOTE]
> - Coded and tested on 1.19

- 🗡️ **Nail:** You start the game with an iron sword + more damage with iron swords + when hitting an entity with an iron sword both of you get a little bit of recoil
- 🔦 **Lumafly lantern:** You start the game with a lantern + If you have a lantern offhand you gain immunity to the darkness effect
- 🐚 **Shell:** You have 5 hearts + Gain an additional heart when gaining some advancements *(`enter the nether`, `kill the dragon`, `kill the wither`, `summon the dragon`)* + Depending on the amount of damage taken, you receive 2, 4 or 16 damage + You always have equipped an skeleton skull + can't wear items that provide armor *(you still can use elytras)*
- 🔮 **Void:** You are pitch black.. that's it :)
- 🧒 **Short:** You are 65% of a human's height (moves normal in 1.5 blocks + shift allows you to move through 1 block tunnels)
- 🚫 **No need for food:** Don't need to eat + disabled natural regeneration *(You can still eat golden food and chorus fruits)*
- 🪶 **Fall immunity:** Fall damage immunity + 1s blindness *(like being a little bit stunned when falling from high :D)*
- 🌆 **Hallownest:** You spawn at an ancient city


### Gem keeper
You attune yourself to different gems making use of an underestimated but powerful resource, light.

> [!NOTE]
> - Coded and tested on 1.19
> - Requires the "eggolib" mod (only for redstone's active)

⭐ **Attunement \[RMB]** - You attune yourself with one of 5 gems
- Your primary and possitives depend on your current gem

**Gems**
- **Amethyst ->** Agile
    - Increased movement and attack speeds
    - Hurl your gemstone forwards and then TP to it
- **Diamond ->** Brawler
    - More armor and passive thorns
    - Burn your foes as well as marking and weakening them
- **Emerald ->** Versatile
    - Hero of the village and Haste
    - Hurl your gemstone forwards and pull nearby entities to it
- **Lapis ->** Mage
    - More xp gain, which is consumed to heal you when needed
    - Make your foes levitate for few seconds and mark them
- **Redstone ->** Utility
    - No passive so far
    - Activate (or deactivate) nearby buttons, levers, pressure plates, observers...


🎒 **Bag of holding \[Secondary]** - Extra inventory
- Gems (and their block variants) aren't dropped on death

☀️ **Light** - You gain light while being under direct sunlight (or moonlight). You use it to maintain the bond between you and your gemstone.
- You consume light when you use a \[Primary]

💔 **Fragile** - You have less health than a usual human

🚫 **Unwieldy** - You are unable to use shields

\***Gemstone:** You always have an invisible armor stand (custom head that shows up your current mode) following you. Most actives interact with it in some way

### Moya's grimoire
This datapack adds 3 additional origin layers
- Glyph -> Small passive effects
- Spell -> Secondary-key actives. Medium tier. All of them have a cost in XP points
- Cantrip -> Load-Toolbar-Activator actives. Utility. You need 5 xp levels to cast them (not consumed). Some of them have no restrictions

🪄 **Glyphs**
- **Abjuration ->** Some extra armor points
- **Celerity ->** 20% buff to movement and attack speeds
- **Secrecy ->** Prevent your armor from being rendered
- **Plague ->** Immunity to the hunger and poison effects
- **Wisdom ->** 20% more XP gain
- **Light ->** Deal 20% to entities that are glowing

🪄 **Spells**
- **Celero ->** Temporary buff to your movement and attack speeds
- **Fireball ->** Summon a fireball which you can then punch to send it forward
- **Incitus ->** You dash in the direction you are looking
- **Magic missile ->** Fire a spectral arrow *(can't be picked up)*
- **Omnia ->** Gain the ability to cast a random spell once
- **Annihilate ->** Summon a non-destroying explosion at your location

🪄 **Cantrips**
- **Conspicio ->** See entities through walls for a brief amount of time
- **Cornum ->** Make the same sound as if you interacted with a horn *(yes, anybody in a 255 block radius will hear it)*
- **Innectis ->** You open a crafting table without needing one
- **Ludus ->** You make the sound of a creeper about to explode
- **Fornix ->** extra inventory that won't drop on death *(5 slots)*
- **Aguamenty ->** Place water :D


**Notes:**
- You can choose to not have a glyph / spell / cantrip if you want
- If you just use the 10 base origins + this pack, there would be around 2160 different possible combinations!
- Better if used in a server with more people :D


### Main character 1
*You play as the main character of a musical.*

🎼 **Metronome **
You always hear a 60bpm metronome.
- *\[Toggle with Sneak + Tab]*

⏰ **Tempo (Max. 3)**
Keeping the tempo up will result in amazing performances. *Just try not to draw too much atention!*
- \[Secondary] ||*Is widely recommended to bound this ability to an accessible key, such as the Middle Mouse Button*||
- While on 3 streak points, you will have the glowing effect
- You lose streak if:
    - Deal / Receive damage
    - Use certain features of the origin
    - Fail this active ability

📈 **Crescendo**
Keeping the tempo boosts you up!
- Speed boost: 10%+ / 10%+ / 20%+
- Armor boost: 1+ / 1+ / 2+
- Damage boost: 1+ / 1+ / 2+

🕺  **Freestyle**
While freestyling, you don't need to keep the tempo up!
- \[Primary]
- For the next 3s, streak will not be consumed. *Then you will lose all your streak*
- Requires a streak of 3

💞 **Boosting ballad**
Boost everyone around you with this romantic piece.
- Keeping the tempo up while on 3 streak will heal 1 HP  and apply haste to every entity within an 8 block radius.

🩰 **Octave jump**
Thrust yourself by doubling the pitch.
- \[Jump]
- Trade 1 streak point for an extra jump.

💔 **Fragile**
You have less health than normal. Also, armor is less effective on you.
- -2 to health and armor points

🚫 **Unwieldy**
You are unable to use shields

To use any of these origins, just download the folder of the origin you want and add it into you world's datapacks folder.
